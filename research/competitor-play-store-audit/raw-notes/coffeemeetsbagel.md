## Full description (verbatim, for internal analysis only)
Opens with three all-caps arrow-bulleted hook lines (► JOIN COFFEE MEETS BAGELS DATING APP / ► SAFE
PLACE TO MEET ONLINE / ► FOR SERIOUS DATERS) that front-load the core positioning claim before any
narrative copy. Leads with a strong stat-based social-proof hook ("91% of our daters are looking for a
serious relationship") and a cumulative usage number ("150 million matches and counting"). Body is
organized into four purple-heart-bulleted (💜) feature sections with bolded mini-headlines (CURATED
COMMUNITY, DETAILED PROFILES, CHATS MADE FOR MEETING, MORE WAYS TO MEET), each 2-3 sentences of
benefit-oriented copy rather than a plain feature list. A clearly separated "MEET CMB PREMIUM" section
discloses the paid tier by name and lists 7 checkmark-bulleted (✔️) premium perks, quantifying a value
claim ("Premium members get up to 2x more dates") without giving explicit price/renewal terms. Closes
with a curated-press-quote block (Yahoo Finance, Mashable x2, Women's Health) as third-party
credibility, then a standard footer of social links, support contact, and ToS/privacy URLs. Emoji use
is moderate and purposeful (section markers, not decorative filler) compared to peers.

## What's new
Announces three specific named features rather than generic "bug fixes" copy: "Topic Suggestions"
(AI-assisted first-message prompts based on the match's profile, piloting in Singapore), "Headlines"
(a short personality tagline under the user's name), and "Real Dates" (first-date idea suggestions) —
plus a closing line noting general improvements and bug fixes.

## Notable ASO/attractiveness components observed
- Title and subtitle both keyword-stack "dating app" / "match" / "date" for search relevance while the
  subtitle explicitly differentiates on intent ("serious daters") rather than volume — a narrower
  positioning bet than Tinder's broad "make friends, meet people, or find a date."
  "Editors' Choice" badge is used in place of a numeric rank badge — likely a stronger trust signal
  since it's a curated Google Play distinction, not just a category leaderboard position.
- Heavy use of a specific 91%-daters statistic and a "150 million matches" cumulative number for
  credibility/social proof, reinforced further by named press-quote block (Yahoo Finance, Mashable,
  Women's Health) — third-party validation is more prominent here than in competitor descriptions.
  Premium tier is named and its perks disclosed in detail, but without explicit price or subscription
  auto-renewal legal language visible in the description (lighter-touch monetization disclosure than
  Tinder's).
- Overall rating (3.2) is noticeably lower than most competitors despite the Editors' Choice badge and
  5M+ installs — worth flagging as a listing that "looks" premium/curated on ASO surface elements but
  carries weaker underlying review sentiment; reviews sampled cite pricing complaints and slow/limited
  match delivery cadence as recurring pain points.
- Screenshots 3-5 repeatedly surface "ID Verified" and "Selfie Verified" badges directly on profile
  cards shown in the UI screenshots — a concrete visual trust signal reinforcing the "safe place to
  meet online" claim from the description's opening hook line, rather than just asserting safety in
  text.
